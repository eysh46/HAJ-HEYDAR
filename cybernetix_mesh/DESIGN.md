## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an uncompromising, high-security cybernetic aesthetic tailored for advanced network privacy, telemetry, and encrypted infrastructure. It merges the technical precision of command-and-control operations with the kinetic energy of electric neon cyber blues and deep midnight sapphire tones.

The visual direction draws from high-contrast technological minimalism and glassmorphic depth:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep midnight voids juxtaposed with concentrated photon illumination. Signals feel energized, encrypted, and instantaneous.
- **Emotion:** Absolute cryptographic security, advanced technical sovereignty, razor-sharp focus, and zero latency.
- **Visual Discipline:** Precision vector boundaries, focused luminescent telemetry, fine structural hairline rules, and purposeful glowing indicators replacing conventional dashboard conventions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an aggressive, luminous hierarchy set strictly against high-absorption dark voids.

- **Primary Canvas & Voids:** `#0a0d14` serves as the canvas substrate, paired with `#0f172a` for elevated structural containers and panels. Deeper surfaces (`#06080d`) provide recessed data layers.
- **Electric Cyber Core (Primary):** `#00f0ff` operates as the primary focal tone for active connections, critical telemetry beacons, interactive states, and glowing data signals.
- **Sapphire Vectors (Secondary & Tertiary):** `#3b82f6` and `#1d4ed8` ground secondary actions, connection paths, and encrypted channel indicators. `#60a5fa` renders secondary typography, high-frequency metrics, and hover micro-states.
- **Status & Telemetry Override:** All positive, healthy, and encrypted network indicators utilize `#00f0ff` and `#60a5fa` with luminescent glows, eliminating generic emerald greens in favor of a coherent electric blue cybernetic matrix. Warnings deploy deep amber-amber-cyan shifts, while critical breaches utilize sharp crimson.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Space Grotesk**, exploiting its mechanical geometry, technical letterforms, and crisp rendering in high-contrast dark environments.

- **Numerics and Metric Values:** Use uppercase positioning and tighter tracking for bandwidth, ping rates, cryptographic handshakes, and IP masks.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** `label-sm` and `label-md` are styled in uppercase with tracking between `+0.08em` and `+0.12em` to echo tactical console readouts.
- **Hierarchy:** Primary headings command focus via high-contrast `#ffffff` with subtle outer cyan drop-highlights on interactive states, while body copy resolves into high-legibility slate `#94a3b8` and `#cbd5e1`.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile), locked to an 8-point base metric rhythm.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`)
  - Tablet: `640px - 1024px` (8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, max-width container `1440px`, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`)
- **Rhythm & Structure:** Dense network graphs and telemetric feeds nest within structured quadrants separated by strict token increments. Visual breathing room is preserved around hero status toggles through `space-xl` buffers to keep mission-critical metrics prominent.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects classic muddy drop shadows in favor of luminous backplates, dark structural tiers, and neon photonic blooming.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#0a0d14` background with an optional faint 24px micro-grid pattern etched in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panels & Structural Modules):** `#0f172a` filled with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.7)` backdrop blur (16px blur) and a crisp 1px perimeter border of `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.18)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Overlay Drawers):** `#111c35` with 24px backdrop blur, bordered by `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)`. Outer glow: `0 0 20px -5px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Focused & Active Engaged States):** Hairline border `1px solid #00f0ff`. Dual-ring luminescence:
  - Inner glow: `inset 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`
  - Outer photon bloom: `0 0 24px 2px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25), 0 0 48px -4px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)`

## Shapes

The system implements a tactical, low-radius shape profile (`roundedness: 1`). Soft curves (0.25rem base, scaling to 0.5rem on cards and 0.75rem on modal viewports) balance engineering precision with contemporary glass ergonomics. 

Pill shapes are reserved strictly for high-priority status badges, connection switches, and latency micro-chips. Decorative shapes favor geometric cuts, angled border-radius trims, and crisp 1px structural framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber Action:** Background `#00f0ff`, text `#0a0d14` with font weight `600`. Outer glow: `0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`. On hover, scale to 1.01 with intensified glow `0 0 24px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)`. Active state shifts to `#60a5fa`.
- **Secondary Sapphire Outline:** Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`, border `1px solid #3b82f6`, text `#60a5fa`. On hover, border flashes `#00f0ff` with subtle cyan fill `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost Action:** Background transparent, text `#94a3b8`, hover state reveals `#ffffff` text and hairline border `rgba(96, 165, 250, 0.3)`.

### VPN Kill-Switch & Connect Toggles
- High-visibility tactile thumb switches. Disconnected state: `#0f172a` container with `#475569` border. Connected state: saturated `#00f0ff` fill with radial pulse animation, glowing `0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.7)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Encapsulated pills (`roundedness: 3`) featuring `label-sm` monospace uppercase typography.
- Secure Tunnel Active: Background `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`, text `#00f0ff`, accompanied by a solid `#00f0ff` 6px glowing indicator dot.

### Cards & Telemetry Nodes
- Dark glass containers (`#0f172a` at 85% opacity, backdrop-filter `blur(12px)`).
- Edge treatment: 1px top border gradient transitioning from `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` to `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.05)`.
- Interactive cards elevate on hover with a cyan perimeter flare and internal sapphire ambient light.

### Inputs & Terminal Fields
- Dark recessed fill `#070a10` with border `1px solid rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)`.
- Text color `#ffffff`, placeholder `#475569`.
- Focus state: Border instantly snaps to `#00f0ff` with `0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)` drop bloom.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with 2px corner radius. Inactive: border `1px solid #334155`, background `#0a0d14`.
- Active/Checked: Fill `#00f0ff`, check vector `#0a0d14` bold mark, encased in a miniature cyan luminescence halo.

### Node Lists & Latency Tables
- Segmented data rows with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` dividers.
- Hover activates a directional gradient scanning effect from left to right using `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.04)`.
- Ping values < 30ms highlighted in `#00f0ff`; node locations prefixed by sapphire territory flags.
