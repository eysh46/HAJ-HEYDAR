## Brand & Style

This design system draws its aesthetic identity from the monumental majesty of Persepolis (*Takht-e Jamshid*), the royal seat of the Achaemenid Empire. Blending high-security cyber-defense architecture with ancient Persian imperial dignity, the interface establishes an atmosphere of indomitable security, ancient sovereignty, and refined luxury.

The target audience seeks elite digital privacy, sovereign encryption, and high-performance routing wrapped in an interface that feels like an artifact of imperial power rather than an ephemeral piece of utility software. Every interaction must project uncompromising resilience, quiet authority, and precision craftsmanship.

### Visual Style Movement
The style operates at the intersection of **Neo-Antiquity Luxury** and **Dark Obsidian Minimalism**:
- **Basalt Monolith Surfaces:** Deep, non-reflective obsidian textures evoking polished black Persepolis stone, carved reliefs, and monumental staircases.
- **Architectural Linear Discipline:** Structural accents mimicking the vertical fluting of Achaemenid columns, frieze paneling, and chiselled lapidary gridlines.
- **Chased Gold Inlay:** Fine hair-width metallic gold stroke lines, radiant active states, and controlled lapis lazuli aura glows that suggest royal gold leaf set into dark masonry.
- **Imperial Geometry:** Subtly integrated motifs inspired by the Persian twelve-petal lotus rosettes, winged guardians, and cuneiform precision, manifested as functional technical glyphs and status radials.

## Colors

The palette balances monumental stone neutrality with ancient royal opulence.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary — Imperial Persian Gold (`#e6b447`):** The sacred metallic signature of the royal court. Used for primary CTAs, active connection rings, critical status indicators, and hairline borders.
  - Hover / Radiant: `#f3ca65`
  - Pressed / Deep: `#c59324`
  - Subtle Gold Wash: `rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.08)`
- **Secondary — Lapis Lazuli (`#1b3b6f`):** The revered stone of imperial treasures. Employed for secondary controls, encrypted protocol indicators, and deep ambient atmospheric backdrops.
  - Elevated Royal Blue: `#274c8b`
  - Shield / Encrypted State Wash: `rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.25)`
- **Tertiary — Persian Turquoise (`#14b8a6`):** The protective talismanic gemstone of Persian jewelry and royal sigils. Serves as the positive verification token (tunnel active, ping nominal, zero leaks).
  - Deep Ancient Teal: `#0f766e`
  - Shimmer Accent: `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`
- **Neutral — Obsidian Basalt (`#0d0e12`):** The monumental dark stone foundations of Persepolis.
  - Canvas / Foundation: `#0d0e12`
  - Basalt Layer 1 (Surface Containers): `#14161d`
  - Basalt Layer 2 (Cards / Modals): `#1a1d26`
  - Basalt Elevated (Hover / Headers): `#232733`
  - Basalt Chiselled Border: `rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.18)`

### Functional Semantics
- **Secure / Connected:** Persian Turquoise (`#14b8a6`) paired with Imperial Gold (`#e6b447`).
- **Connecting / Negotiation:** Lapis Lazuli (`#274c8b`) breathing pulse.
- **Alert / Severed:** Antique Imperial Rust (`#c44536`) with gold-tinted containment borders.
- **Typography Tone:** High-clarity ivory white (`#f4f4f5`) for headers, warm alabaster (`#d1d3dc`) for body, and muted bronze stone (`#8e93a6`) for telemetry and labels.

## Typography

Typography embodies mathematical architecture, geometric rigor, and monumental inscription. **Space Grotesk** delivers modern utilitarian technical clarity balanced with historical geometric chiseled forms reminiscent of stonemasons' lettercraft.

For Persian/Arabic scripts (such as regional node labels or multilingual user toggles), the layout renders in **Vazirmatn**, matching the vertical metric scale, baseline stability, and dignified weight of Space Grotesk.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Imperial Uppercase Tracking:** All micro-labels, protocol identifiers, node IDs, and connection status badges must use uppercase styling paired with expanded letter-spacing (`label-sm` or `label-lg`) to evoke stone monument engravings.
- **Numerical Stature:** Latency metrics, IP values, and throughput counters must render with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure zero jitter during real-time data streaming.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the **Achaemenid Apadana Palace**: a columned, structured, and monumental framework that emphasizes balance, solemn rhythm, and unambiguous order.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column structured layout with wide `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Primary dashboard widgets, telemetry graphs, and server lists align cleanly along rigid architectural axes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins. Secondary telemetry panels tuck into collapsed accordions or side drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `1rem` margins and fluid components. The grand imperial connect trigger anchors the central visual field.

### Spacing Philosophy
Layout distances strictly use the spacing scale tokens:
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compact internal telemetry badges and status pill chips.
- Use `space-md` for standard card padding and form element groupings.
- Use `space-lg` for section modular dividers and primary button interior padding.
- Use `space-xl` for heroic spacing around the central power node and dashboard macro sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on generic drop shadows, but on **Chiselled Basalt Tiers** and **Chased Gold Inlay Lighting**. Depth corresponds directly to protective layering and cryptographic focus.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Foundation Bedrock):** `#0d0e12` — Deep non-reflective slate stone with an imperceptible radial vignette.
2. **Level 1 (Relief Slabs):** `#14161d` — Encapsulated cards and telemetry panes bounded by hair-thin bronze-gold perimeter seams (`1px solid rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.16)`).
3. **Level 2 (Active Monoliths):** `#1a1d26` — Interactive elements, focused server cards, and modal sheets.
4. **Level 3 (Imperial Prominence):** `#232733` with gold leaf border gradient highlights (`linear-gradient(135deg, #e6b447 0%, rgba(230,180,71,0.2) 50%, #1b3b6f 100%)`).

### Illumination & Ambient Energy
- **The Imperial Aura:** Key interactive elements (such as the main tunnel switch) cast an ambient, non-directional atmospheric corona: `box-shadow: 0 0 32px -4px rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.28), 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Lapis Shield Glow:** Encrypted tunnel states introduce an interior radial wash: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.35) 0%, transparent 70%)`.
- **Persepolis Fluting Accents:** Section dividers leverage vertical or horizontal fluting: alternating 1px stroke lines of dark basalt (`#232733`) and translucent gold (`rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft Lapidary Corner** (`roundedness: 1`). Monolithic architecture is defined by chiseled stone blocks and carved granite lintels, not playful bubbles or harsh raw brutalism.

- Standard UI containers and cards utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner rounding, maintaining the crispness of precision masonry.
- Hero cards and bottom sheets use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Modals and focal containers utilize `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- **The Sole Exception:** The Imperial Connection Sigil and icon medallions adopt circular geometry (`rounded-full`), representing the ancient Achaemenid solar disks and rosette medallions of Persepolis.

## Components

### 1. Imperial Master Switch (VPN Connect Trigger)
- **Structure:** A prominent circular medallion resting in a layered basalt bezel. The central sigil features the stylized imperial lotus rosette or Faravahar wing geometry.
- **States:**
  - *Disconnected:* Basalt slate face (`#1a1d26`) with a muted antique gold border (`#c59324` at 40% opacity). Label: "INITIALIZE BINDING".
  - *Negotiating:* Dual-ring rotation with Lapis Lazuli pulse animation.
  - *Connected:* Burnished Gold surface accent (`#e6b447`) with a Persian Turquoise status beacon (`#14b8a6`). Outward gold-amber atmospheric glow.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Imperial Button:** Filled with gradient Imperial Gold (`linear-gradient(180deg, #f3ca65 0%, #d4a034 100%)`), text in obsidian black (`#0d0e12`), bold weight, uppercase tracking. Beveled edge highlighted with `1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary (Lapis Vault Button):** Deep royal blue background (`#1b3b6f`), antique gold border (`1px solid rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.3)`), ivory text. Hover state brightens to `#274c8b` with an intensified gold stroke.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, muted gold text (`#e6b447`), bordered only on hover with subtle basalt elevation.

### 3. Server Node Cards & Lists
- **Container:** Basalt `#14161d` with a 1px border of `rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.14)`.
- **Selected State:** Border lights up to solid `#e6b447`; left edge features a 3px vertical accent bar reminiscent of a royal pillar flute.
- **Latency Indicator:** Numerical ping displayed with tabular numerals, accented by a small Persian Turquoise rosette beacon for pings under 40ms, transitioning to antique gold, then rust.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** Recessed basalt well (`#0d0e12`) with `1px solid rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.2)`.
- **Placeholder:** Muted stone tone (`#8e93a6`).
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to radiant gold (`#e6b447`) with an ambient halo (`0 0 12px rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.25)`).

### 5. Chips & Protocol Badges
- **Visuals:** Compact chips with 4px radii. Background in `#1a1d26`, hairline gold border (`rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.25)`). Text styled with `label-sm` tracking.
- **Active / Verified Protocols (e.g., VLESS, Shadowsocks):** Turquoise text with faint teal ambient backing (`rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.12)`).

### 6. Toggle Controls & Radio Selectors
- **Track:** Deep basalt stone (`#0d0e12`) framed in low-opacity bronze (`rgba(230, 180, 71, 0.3)`).
- **Thumb:** Polished gold coin (`#e6b447`) with a micro-embossed center dot.

### 7. Telemetry & Cryptographic Data Cards
- Live traffic graphs are rendered in fine hairline gold vectors (`#e6b447`) over deep blue gridlines (`rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.4)`), reminiscent of ancient Persian star charts and celestial navigation.
